Questions
Good questions

      

        
Does it work with a single monitor?
LookFocus is built for two or more displays: it decides which screen gets the keyboard. With one display there's nothing to switch between.

        
Do I need a fancy camera?
No. The built-in FaceTime camera or any USB webcam works. It prefers an external webcam if you have one plugged in.

        
Why does it need Accessibility permission?
macOS only lets an app bring another app's window to the front with Accessibility access. LookFocus uses it for exactly that, plus noticing when you're typing so it can wait.

        
Is it accurate?
Switching between monitors is reliable after a quick calibration, and it improves as you click around. Glances under about a third of a second are ignored on purpose, so a peek doesn't steal focus.

        
Does it slow my Mac down?
It analyses a few frames a second with Apple's Vision framework, and the camera switches off whenever you pause, lock, or sleep.

        
What about Windows?
Mac only for now. If enough people ask, a Windows version might happen.

        
Can I get a refund?
If it doesn't work on your setup, email within 14 days and you'll get your $1.99 back. No forms, no fuss.
